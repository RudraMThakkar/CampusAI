2. ABOUT THE INSTITUTE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Kilachand Devchand Polytechnic (commonly known as K.D. Polytechnic) was established in the year 1961 at Patan, Gujarat, India.
- Objective: To provide quality technical education to students of North Gujarat.
- It is a state government polytechnic that has played a significant role in producing skilled diploma engineers to meet the needs of industry and society.
- It offers AICTE-approved diploma programs in four engineering specializations, designed to align with current industrial requirements and technological advancements.
- It is affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU), Ahmedabad, ensuring standardized academic practices and quality education.
- Recognized as one of the premier technical education institutes in North Gujarat. It emphasizes holistic development by encouraging freedom of thought, innovation, and ethical responsibility, and nurtures social awareness, environmental concern, and professional discipline among students.
- Program duration: 3 Years Diploma (full time).

20. APPLIED MECHANICS DEPARTMENT (SUPPORT DEPARTMENT FOR CIVIL & MECHANICAL)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- About: Applied Mechanics is a core engineering discipline that applies principles of mechanics to solve real-world problems in structures, materials, and fluids. It bridges theoretical physics with practical applications in civil and mechanical engineering.
- Core Focus: Statics (forces in equilibrium), dynamics (motion and acceleration), and strength of materials (stress, strain, deformation). Essential for diploma students of civil or mechanical engineering to analyze beams, trusses, friction, and centroids. Labs emphasize experiments like verifying laws of motion and testing material properties.
- Department strengths: Strong foundation in engineering concepts; Well-equipped laboratories (Applied Mechanics Laboratory with testing equipment, material testing and strength analysis facilities, demonstration models); Practical and skill-based learning (hands-on experiments, real-life problem solving, mini projects); Experienced, student-friendly faculty.
- Key Disciplines Covered:
  * Engineering Mechanics: Fundamental knowledge of forces, motion, equilibrium, and mechanics principles; analytical and problem-solving skills for civil engineering; supports competitive exam and technical interview preparation.
  * Mechanics of Structures: Structural behavior under different loads; stress, strain, bending, shear force, and deflection; analysis of beams, columns, trusses, and structural members.
  * Soil Engineering: Soil properties and behavior; soil classification, permeability, compaction, and consolidation; foundation design; soil testing and field investigation methods.
  * Concrete Technology: Properties of concrete ingredients; cement, aggregates, water, and admixtures; safe, economical, and sustainable construction practices.
  * Design of Structures: Design principles for beams, slabs, columns, footings, and other structural elements; standard codes and engineering design practices.

21. SCIENCE & HUMANITIES DEPARTMENT (FIRST YEAR DEPARTMENT)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- About: The Department of Science and Humanities serves as the vital foundation of any Diploma Engineering institute and is often referred to as the "First Year Department". It bridges secondary school education and specialized technical engineering, and equips students with the fundamental principles that underpin every engineering discipline.
- Core Purpose: To nurture a scientific temperament and provide students with the analytical tools they need for professional subjects (physics, mathematics, and logic).
- Key Disciplines Covered:
  * Engineering Mathematics: Algebra, trigonometry, and calculus - the "language of engineering".
  * Engineering Physics: Laws of nature, from mechanics and optics to acoustics and semiconductors.
  * Engineering Chemistry: Material properties, corrosion, water treatment, and lubricants.
  * Communication Skills (English): Professional writing, presentation skills, and verbal fluency.
- GTU Framework: Outcome-based curriculum. The department typically manages the Induction Program, a mandatory GTU initiative to help new students transition into the professional college environment through creative arts, universal human values, and physical activities.
- Laboratories:
  * Applied Physics Lab: Dimension measuring instruments (Vernier Callipers, Micrometer screw gauge, Spherometer), material properties apparatus (Hooke's law, surface tension and viscosity apparatus), centripetal force apparatus, simple pendulum and photogate setup, sound and acoustics apparatus (electrical vibrator/Melde's apparatus), resonance tube apparatus, heat and thermometry apparatus such as linear thermal expansion apparatus.
  * Modern Physics Lab: Dimension measuring instruments, capacitance measuring apparatus (parallel plate capacitor, series and parallel connection of resistance and capacitors), sound and acoustics apparatus, resonance tube apparatus, semiconductor devices (PN junction diode, Zener diode, half wave, full wave and bridge rectifier), logic gates and De Morgan's theorem kit.
  * Chemistry Lab: Modern apparatus for chemical analysis.
  * Language Lab: Software-based training to improve phonetics and soft skills.
- Career Development: Builds logical reasoning, professional ethics, and adaptability for students who move on to Civil, Mechanical, Computer or Electrical branches.
