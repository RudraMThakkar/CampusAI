2. OFFERED BRANCHES (ONLY 4 BRANCHES OFFERED)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
K. D. Polytechnic Patan currently offers ONLY four diploma engineering branches:
1. Computer Engineering
2. Electrical Engineering
3. Mechanical Engineering
4. Civil Engineering

* STRICT NOTE ON IT BRANCH:
Information Technology (IT) branch is NOT offered at K. D. Polytechnic Patan. If any student inquires about IT, clarify that only Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Civil Engineering are available.

10. ADMISSIONS, FEES & SCHOLARSHIPS GROUND TRUTH
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Admission Gateway: Centralized ACPDC Gujarat (https://acpdc.gujarat.gov.in) based on 10th SSC merit.
- Annual Tuition Fees:
  * Boys: Approx. ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 per year.
  * Girls: 100% Tuition Fee Exemption under the Government "Kanya Kelavani" scheme (Completely free tuition).
- Scholarships:
  * MYSY (Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana): 80+ percentile in 10th SSC, annual family income <= ₹6,00,000.
  * Digital Gujarat Portal (https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in): Post-matric scholarships for SC, ST, SEBC/OBC, and EWS students.
- GTU Rules: Minimum 75% attendance mandatory. Max 4 backlogs from Sem 1 & 2 allowed for entry into Sem 5.
